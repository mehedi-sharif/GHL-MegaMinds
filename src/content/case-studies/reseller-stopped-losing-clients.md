  <div class="grid g2" style="gap:40px">
    <div class="card pad">
      <h3>What we ran</h3>

        <li>Account set up fully before it is handed to the client</li>
        <li>Snapshot loaded and checked, not just imported</li>
        <li>Training call once they have something to look at</li>
        <li>Help desk replies in his brand, not ours</li>
        <li>A check in before the second invoice lands</li>
