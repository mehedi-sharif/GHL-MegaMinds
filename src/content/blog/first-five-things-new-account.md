Everyone opens a fresh GoHighLevel account and reaches straight for workflows. That is the wrong first move. Workflows are the plumbing — they only matter once water is flowing.

## Build these first

1. **A calendar with real booking rules.** One working booking link beats ten half-built automations.
2. **A single lead form** that feeds one pipeline. Keep it to name, email, phone.
3. **Missed call text back.** The fastest thing in the whole platform to pay for itself.
4. **A two message follow up** — one text, one email — that goes out the moment a lead comes in.
5. **A review request** you can fire off after a job.

Do these five and you have value in week one. Everything else can wait until you actually feel the pinch.
